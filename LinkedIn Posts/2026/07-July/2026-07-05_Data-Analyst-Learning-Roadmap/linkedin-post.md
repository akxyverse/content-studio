The truth is...

👉 There isn't a shortcut. There is a roadmap.

Here's a practical learning path:
🟢 Excel – Learn the fundamentals of working with data.
 🔵 SQL – Query, filter, and analyze data from databases.
 🐍 Python – Clean, automate, and explore data efficiently.
 📊 Power BI / Tableau – Turn insights into impactful dashboards.
 📚 Statistics – Understand the "why" behind the numbers.
 ☁️ Cloud Basics – Learn how modern organizations store and manage data.
 🏗️ Data Warehousing & ETL – Understand how data is collected and prepared.
 🤖 Machine Learning Basics – Explore predictive analytics and AI fundamentals.
 📂 Projects & Portfolio – Apply your knowledge to real-world problems.
 💬 Communication & Business Thinking – Transform insights into meaningful decisions.
