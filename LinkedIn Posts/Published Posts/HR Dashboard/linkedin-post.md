HR teams often manage employee information in spreadsheets, making it difficult to quickly answer business questions such as:

➜ Which department has the highest payroll?
➜ Which city has the largest employee workforce?
➜ What is the average employee salary?
➜ Who are the highest and lowest-paid employees?
➜ How can all these insights be viewed from a single dashboard?
Instead of manually filtering spreadsheets, I built a dashboard that delivers these insights in just a few clicks.
