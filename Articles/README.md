## 📐 Article Format Standard

Every article in every subject folder follows the same shape, so a reader can scan any article in the same way regardless of topic:

1. **Metadata card**, right under the title:
   ```markdown
   > 📅 Published: <Month Year>
   > ⏱ Reading Time: <N> min
   > 🎯 Level: <e.g. Beginner → Intermediate>
   > 🏷 Category: <Subject>
   > 💼 Relevant Roles: <who this is for>
   > 📚 Related Guide: <the closest guide in data-analytics-resources, if one exists>
   ```
2. **📌 Key Takeaways box** — 3-5 bullet summary right after the metadata card, before the full article, so the core point lands in 10 seconds even if the reader doesn't read further.
3. The existing structure (Overview → Why It Matters → concept/examples → Common Mistakes → Best Practices → Related Resources) stays as-is.
4. **Continue Learning**, standardized as the closing section: arrow-bulleted (`➡`) links to the most relevant guide, the next article in the same subject collection, and any other genuinely relevant repo — not a fixed list copy-pasted into every article regardless of relevance.
